SoundDeck 1.4
=============

A soundboard for Windows 10 and 11. It plays sounds through your microphone into Discord, games and OBS, and you can trigger them with hotkeys.


GETTING STARTED
---------------

1. Put this folder somewhere permanent, for example in Documents, then double-click SoundDeck.exe.
   No installation is needed.

   If Windows says "Windows protected your PC", click "More info" and then "Run anyway".
   That warning appears because the app isn't code-signed. It's normal for small, free apps.

2. In the sidebar on the right, click "Install virtual mic". You only need to do this once.
   SoundDeck downloads VB-CABLE, a free virtual audio cable, from its official website.
   Approve the Windows prompt and click "Install Driver". Restart your PC if it asks you to.

3. In Discord, go to Settings > Voice & Video and set Input Device to "CABLE Output".
   Keep "Mix my microphone in" turned on in SoundDeck, so people still hear your voice.

4. Also in Voice & Video, turn these OFF:
     - Noise Suppression: None   (Krisp removes sound effects and music along with noise)
     - Echo Cancellation: off
     - Automatic Gain Control: off
   You don't lose noise removal: SoundDeck's "Remove background noise" (on by default)
   cleans up your own microphone before the sounds are mixed in, so only your voice is
   filtered, never the sounds.

5. Do you use push-to-talk in Discord? In the sidebar, click "Push-to-talk key" and press the
   same key (or mouse button) you use in Discord. SoundDeck then holds it down while a sound
   plays, so your friends hear it.


USING IT
--------

  Add sounds      Click "Add sounds", or drag audio files or folders onto the window.
                  Supported formats: MP3, WAV, M4A, AAC, WMA and FLAC.
  Play / stop     Click a pad to play it. Click it again to stop it.
  Hotkeys         Right-click a pad and choose "Set hotkey". Hotkeys work in games too.
  Trim / loop     Right-click a pad > "Trim, loop and play mode" to cut the start or end
                  (the file itself isn't changed), make it loop, or choose what pressing it
                  again does: stop it, start it over, or play another copy on top.
  Even loudness   "Even out loudness" in the sidebar (on by default) makes quiet and loud
                  clips play at about the same level. Per-sound volume still works on top.
  Stop everything Click "Stop all", press Esc, or set a stop-all hotkey in the sidebar.
  Search          Press Ctrl+F, then press Enter to play the first match.
                  Search looks through all categories.


RECORDING SOUNDS
----------------

Click "Record" at the top, or press Ctrl+R. You can record from:

  Microphone      Uses the microphone that's selected in the sidebar.
  Computer audio  Records everything playing on your PC, such as a YouTube clip or a
                  game sound. Start recording, play the clip, then stop.

Press the red button or Space to start and stop. Silence at the start and the end is
cut off automatically. Drag the purple handles to trim more, and press Preview (or Space)
to listen. Only you hear the preview. Give the sound a name, pick a category, and
click "Save to board". Recordings are saved as .wav files in Documents\SoundDeck Recordings.
A recording can be up to 5 minutes long.

Instant replay: turn on "Keep the last 30 s of PC audio" in the sidebar and set a
save-replay hotkey. When something funny happens, press the hotkey and you get the last
30 seconds to trim and save. The audio is only kept in memory until you save it.


ORGANIZING MANY SOUNDS
----------------------

  Reorder         Drag a pad to a new spot.
  Categories      Click "+ New category" above the pads. To move a sound into a category,
                  drag it onto the category's tab, or right-click it > Move to category.
                  Right-click a tab to rename it, move it, sort its sounds A-Z or delete it.
                  Deleting a category keeps its sounds; they stay under All.
  Folders         Adding a whole folder from the All tab turns it into a category with
                  the folder's name. Sounds you add while a category is open go into it.
  Favorites       Click the star on a pad. Your favorites get their own tab.
  Switch tabs     Ctrl+1 to Ctrl+9.
  Volume          Right-click a pad > Volume to make one sound louder or quieter (0-200%).
  Compact view    The button to the right of the tabs fits many more sounds on screen.
  Select several  Ctrl+click or Shift+click pads (Ctrl+A selects all), then right-click for
                  favorite / category / remove. Delete removes them.
  Undo            Removed something by accident? Press Ctrl+Z.
  Moved files     If a sound says "Can't load", right-click it > "Locate the file". Other
                  missing sounds from the same folder are found automatically.
  Share a board   "Export board to share" (sidebar) packs your sounds into one .sdboard file.
                  Your friends use Add sounds > "Import a shared board".


APP SETTINGS
------------

  Start with Windows     Starts SoundDeck when you log in.
  Keep running in tray   Closing the window keeps SoundDeck running next to the clock, so
                         hotkeys and your mic keep working. Right-click the icon to quit.
  Updates                SoundDeck checks GitHub for a new version when it starts. If there
                         is one, an "Update" button appears at the top: one click installs it
                         and restarts. Your sounds and settings are kept.
  Headsets               Plugging audio devices in or out is picked up automatically.

SoundDeck must be running for your voice to reach Discord while Discord's input is set to
CABLE Output. When you're done, close SoundDeck and switch Discord back to your normal mic.

Your sounds and settings are saved in %APPDATA%\SoundDeck.
The sound files themselves stay where they are. If you move them, use "Locate the file".
